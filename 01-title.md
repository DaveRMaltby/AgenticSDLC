## What is the Agentic SDLC?

The Agentic SDLC is a way of building software in which AI agents take an active part in every stage of the software development lifecycle, from planning and design through coding, testing, review, and release. Rather than acting as autocomplete tools, these agents take on whole tasks. They break work down, write and change code, run builds and tests, and check their own results against clear guardrails. Engineers move from doing every step by hand to directing agents, reviewing their output, and making the calls that need human judgment. Quality and verification become part of the process from the start, not a gate at the end.
